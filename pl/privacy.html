Privacy Policy

Last updated July 2026

Your friends and contact data never leave your device. We have no accounts and no advertising. We do collect a small set of anonymous usage counts through TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service in Germany, anonymous purchase records through RevenueCat, and anonymous crash reports through Sentry, to understand how the app is being used, to restore your purchases, and to fix what breaks. We cannot identify you from these, and your friends' names and contact details are never included.

That's it. That's the privacy policy.

If you want the longer version for legal purposes, keep reading.

What data Dear accesses

Contact names and photos. During onboarding, Dear asks for permission to access your Contacts. This is used solely to let you select people to add to your nudge list. Contact data is read once during selection and stored locally on your device. Dear never transmits contact data off your device, and you can use the app without granting Contacts access by typing names manually.

Notification permissions. Dear requests permission to send local notifications so it can remind you to reach out to someone. These notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device; their content is never sent anywhere.

What data Dear collects or transmits

Dear has no accounts and no advertising. We do not use tracking pixels or advertising frameworks. We do not know who you are, who your friends are, or what messages you send. We do collect anonymous usage counts through TelemetryDeck, anonymous purchase records through RevenueCat, and anonymous crash reports through Sentry (see sections below). Beyond that, we know only what Apple and Google provide in aggregate App Store and Google Play analytics, which contains no personally identifiable information and is controlled by your device settings.

What we never track: your name, your friends' names or contact information, what messages you send, the time of day you opened the app, your location, your IP address, or any device identifier that could be linked back to you.

These events are pseudonymous and aggregated. We cannot identify you from them. Because no personal data is processed, we do not require a consent prompt under GDPR. If you would prefer to opt out anyway, please email us at support@dearnudges.com and we will note your request for the next release.

Delete your data

Dear has no accounts, and no server holds your personal information. Your friends, your nudges, and your messages live only on your device. To delete everything Dear knows about you:

In-app purchases

Dear offers optional subscriptions and one-time purchases through the Apple App Store (and Google Play on Android). Apple and Google handle all payment processing; we never see or store your card or payment details. To unlock what you've bought across devices and reinstalls, Dear uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous record of each transaction (the product purchased, the purchase date, and an anonymous app-internal identifier). This record contains no name, email, or payment information. The privacy policies of Apple, Google, and RevenueCat govern this data.

Third-party services

The Dear app uses four third-party services. Apple StoreKit and Google Play Billing process in-app purchases. RevenueCat manages purchase entitlements and receives the anonymous transaction records described above. TelemetryDeck (anonymous usage analytics) receives the anonymous event counts described above. Sentry receives crash reports so we can fix what breaks; user identity is stripped from every report before it is sent, and no personal information is attached. No advertising networks, tracking libraries, or data brokers are used.

This website

This policy describes the Dear app. The website you are reading is separate, and it works differently. dearnudges.com has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no tracking scripts. It sets nothing in your browser and asks you for nothing.

Two companies do see your visit, because they deliver the site: GitHub hosts the files, and Cloudflare serves them and handles security. Like any web host, both receive your IP address and browser details in their server logs. We do not control those logs and we do not read them. Fonts are served from this domain rather than a font provider, so no third party is contacted to display this page.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data. The anonymous usage counts sent to TelemetryDeck are not linked to you personally and are not shared with any advertising network or data broker. TelemetryDeck's own privacy policy governs how they process these counts on their infrastructure.

Children's privacy

Dear does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. The anonymous usage counts we collect through TelemetryDeck contain no personal data and cannot identify any individual user, including children.

The Dear Friend post-purchase screen may help you compose an email to Elsewhere Index. This email may include:

  • Tu nombre preferido, si eliges aparecer en los créditos de Dear

This email is composed on your device using your own email app. You review and send it yourself. Dear does not collect, store, or transmit this information through any server or API. Elsewhere Index receives it only through your email and uses it only for the stated purposes.

Your email address is visible to Elsewhere Index when you send the message. It will not be added to any marketing list or shared with anyone else.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how Dear handles data (for example, adding optional cloud sync in a future version), we will update this policy and note the changes here. Any future feature that involves data transmission will be opt-in and clearly disclosed.
