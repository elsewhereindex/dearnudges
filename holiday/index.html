Dear

Flowers for the holidays. A hello for the rest of the year.

Dear is a quiet app for staying close to friends and family. Each day it brings up one or two of your people, with a moment to text or call.

Free for up to 15 people. iPhone, iPad and Android.

One card a day. Your people, not a feed.

  • One or two names a dayNot a to-do list. Just who might like to hear from you today.
  • Text or call in a tapDear opens your messages or phone. You say the rest.
  • Your people stay on your phoneNo accounts, no ads. Names and contacts never leave your device.

You already showed up for the holidays. Dear helps you keep in touch in January, and in June.
