About Dear

Why Dear Exists

Dear was built on a simple observation: we think about our friends more often than we reach out to them. Research shows that people consistently underestimate how much others appreciate hearing from them. Dear closes that gap.

One gentle nudge a day. No guilt, no streaks, no social feed. If you don't reach out today, the person simply appears again another day. The app never tells you that you failed. It just quietly keeps showing up.

The Research Behind It

People appreciate hearing from you more than you think

People consistently underestimate how much others value an unexpected message. The gap between how awkward you think it will be and how warmly it's received is large and consistent across strangers, acquaintances, and old friends.

Liu et al., Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 2022

Your friendships have natural layers

Humans maintain about 5 close people, 15 good friends, and 50 in their wider circle. Dear respects these natural tiers and lets you choose your own rhythm for each person.

Robin Dunbar, Personal Relationships, 2016

Small nudges close the gap between thinking and doing

Specific plans dramatically increase follow-through. "I should text them" becomes "I just did." Dear turns intention into action by giving you a specific person, a specific moment, and a one-tap path to reach out.

Gollwitzer, American Psychologist, 1999

This is a health issue, not just a nice idea

The WHO declared loneliness a pressing global health threat in 2023, reporting that around 1 in 4 older people experience social isolation. Separately, Holt-Lunstad's meta-analysis found social isolation raises the risk of premature death by 26%. Dear is a small tool pointed at a big problem.

WHO Commission on Social Connection, 2023; Holt-Lunstad et al., 2010

How It Works

1

Add your people

Import from contacts or add manually. Set how often you want to reach out to each person: weekly, every two weeks, monthly, or less.

2

Get a daily nudge

Each day, Dear suggests someone to reach out to, delivered in a voice persona you choose. Sherlock's deductions, Nonna's gentle guilt, Cyrano's declarations, or 12 others.

3

Text, call, or FaceTime

One tap opens a text, call, or video chat. That's it. No journaling, no metrics, no noise. Just a real conversation with a real person.

15 Voice Personas

The voice personas make the nudge feel like a friend reminding you, not a calendar alert. Each one writes differently, notices differently, and cares differently.

Private by Design

Your friends, your messages, your relationships: none of it ever leaves your device. No accounts, no ads, nothing sold. The only things Dear sends are anonymous usage counts, anonymous purchase records and crash reports, never anything that could identify you or your people. We designed it this way on purpose.
